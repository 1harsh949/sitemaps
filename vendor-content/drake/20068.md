# Drake Tax - Electronic Filing & Account Information (ERO)

## Overview

An Electronic Return Originator (ERO) must complete ERO setup to e-file returns, download program updates, and access the online e-file database. Access this screen via **Setup > Electronic Filing & Account Information (ERO)**.

Only one ERO is permitted per firm, and ERO information must be completed before e-filing.

## Setup Steps

1. From the Home window menu bar, select **Setup > Electronic Filing & Account Information (ERO)** to open the dialog box.
2. In the **Account Information** section, enter your Account Number, EFIN, and e-File Password (required), plus the ERO name, address, ZIP code, and EIN (optional).
3. If needed, click the **Edit Serial Number** button to make changes; otherwise, leave blank.
4. For franchises or networks, complete the **Franchise/Network Information** fields; otherwise, leave blank.
5. Enter the franchise or network name in the **Disclosure or Use of Tax Information** field if applicable (supports IRC § 7216 compliance).
6. Click **OK**.

## Testing Your Setup

Click the **Test Account** button at the bottom of the dialog box to verify e-file setup functionality. Correct credentials will open the Get Acknowledgments window and test your configuration.
