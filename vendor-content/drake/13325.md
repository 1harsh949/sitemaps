# Drake Tax - Return Due Dates and Deadlines

## Overview

Federal return deadlines vary by form type. Most states align with federal deadlines, though variations exist. Filing compliance is based on e-file transmission time (midnight local time), not IRS receipt. Drake Software recommends e-filing several hours before the deadline.

## Form 1040

- **Standard Due Date:** April 15
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Wednesday, April 15, 2026
- **Form 4868 Extension:** 6 months
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Thursday, October 15, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 5 days

**Exceptions:**
- Farmers/fishermen (≥2/3 income from farming/fishing): March 2, 2026 deadline to avoid estimated tax penalties
- US citizens/resident aliens abroad: June 15, 2026 (or October 15, 2026 with extension)

## Form 706

- **Standard Due Date:** 9 months after decedent's death
- **Form 4768 Extension:** 6 months
- **Perfection Period:** 5 days

## Form 709

- **Standard Due Date:** April 15
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Wednesday, April 15, 2026
- **Form 4868 Extension:** 6 months
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Thursday, October 15, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 5 days

## Form 1041

- **Standard Due Date:** 15th day of 4th month following entity's year-end
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Wednesday, April 15, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 10 days
- **Form 7004 Extension:** 5.5 months (6 months for bankruptcy estate)
- **Extension Perfection Period:** 5 days
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Wednesday, September 30, 2026 (Thursday, October 15, 2026 for bankruptcy estates)

## Form 1120, 1120-C, and 1120-H

- **Standard Due Date:** 15th day of 4th month following entity's year-end
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Wednesday, April 15, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 10 days
- **Form 7004 Extension:** 6 months
- **Extension Perfection Period:** 5 days
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Thursday, October 15, 2026

**Note:** Corporations with fiscal years ending June 30 must file by the 15th day of the 3rd month after year-end per 1120 Instructions.

## Form 1065

- **Standard Due Date:** 15th day of 3rd month following entity's year-end
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Monday, March 16, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 10 days
- **Form 7004 Extension:** 6 months
- **Extension Perfection Period:** 5 days
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Tuesday, September 15, 2026

## Form 1120-S

- **Standard Due Date:** 15th day of 3rd month following entity's year-end
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Monday, March 16, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 10 days
- **Form 7004 Extension:** 6 months
- **Extension Perfection Period:** 5 days
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Tuesday, September 15, 2026

## Form 990

- **Standard Due Date:** 15th day of 5th month following entity's year-end
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Friday, May 15, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 10 days
- **Form 8868 Extension:** 6 months
- **Extension Perfection Period:** 5 days
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Monday, November 16, 2026

## Form 990-T

- **Standard Due Date:** 15th day of 4th or 5th month following entity's year-end
  - Employees' trusts (401(a)), IRAs, Roth IRAs, Coverdell ESAs, Archer MSAs: 4th month
  - All other organizations: 5th month
- **2025 Filing Season Deadline:** Wednesday, April 15, 2026 OR Friday, May 15, 2026
- **Perfection Period:** 10 days
- **Form 8868 Extension:** 6 months
- **Extension Perfection Period:** 5 days
- **Extended Return Deadline:** Thursday, October 15, 2026 OR Monday, November 16, 2026

## Perfection Periods

### Individual Return Perfection Periods

The perfection period for Form 1040 returns and Form 4868 extensions is 5 days. "The perfection period is not an extension" — if not timely filed initially, no perfection period applies. DRKPARSE and Drake B-ack errors do not qualify.

You may re-transmit filings multiple times during the perfection period. Transmissions after the period ends are considered late.

**To paper-file a timely e-filed but rejected return/extension:**

1. File by the later of the return's due date or ten calendar days after IRS rejection notification
2. Include:
   - Explanation of late filing
   - Copy of electronic rejection notification
3. Return or 2350 extension must be signed by taxpayer and preparer (no PIN)

### Business Return or Extension Perfection Periods

The perfection period allows "perfecting" a rejected e-filing. The period begins when a return/extension is timely transmitted and may extend beyond the due date.

**Perfection period lengths:**
- 7004 and 8868 extensions: 5 days
- 1120, 1120-S, 1065, 1041, and 990: 10 days

The IRS "looks back" 5 or 10 days from accepted e-filing date to determine the earliest rejection within that period. If the earliest rejection is timely, the accepted e-filing is considered timely. "The perfection period is never extended regardless of weekends, holidays or the end of the year cutoff."

**To re-transmit a corrected business return/extension:**

1. Figure perfection period from last timely filing
2. Diagnose, correct, and re-transmit
3. Must re-transmit within the perfection period
4. Can re-transmit multiple times
5. Any retransmission after perfection period ends is late

**To paper-file a corrected business return/extension:**

**For a return:**
1. Must have e-file waiver if taxpayer is required to e-file (call IRS e-help Desk: 1-866-255-0654)
2. Must be postmarked by the later of the return's due date (including extensions) or 10 calendar days after IRS rejection notification
3. Include:
   - Explanation of late filing
   - Copy of reject notification
   - Brief history of correction actions
   - "REJECTED ELECTRONIC RETURN - (DATE)" in red at top of first page (date of first reject within perfection period)
   - Taxpayer signature

**For an extension:**
1. Must be postmarked by the later of the return's due date or 5 calendar days after IRS rejection notification (e-file waiver not required)
2. Include:
   - Explanation of late filing
   - Copy of reject notification

**Notes on Required E-filers:**

Notice 2010-13 applies to: partnerships with 100+ K-1s, corporations, S corporations, and tax-exempt organizations with $10 million+ in assets. "Before filing a paper return, corporations, partnerships and tax exempt organizations required to e-file must contact the e-help Desk (1-866-255-0654)."

## Postmark Date

For paper-filed returns, postmark date determines timeliness. Request a manual (local) postmark at a USPS retail location to ensure the postmark date matches the mailing date. Mail placed in receptacles receives a postmark at processing facilities, but the date may differ. For returns mailed near the due date, request a manual postmark to ensure IRS and state acceptance as timely.
