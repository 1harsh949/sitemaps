# Invite clients

## Before you start

Granting clients portal access differs from inviting them to the portal. When you grant access by checking the **Login** box, you can also send an email invitation.

Key considerations before inviting clients:

- Always test with a demo client first to verify all client portal features work properly
- Bulk portal access applies identical settings to all contacts linked to selected accounts
- For selective access, grant permissions individually via the client profile
- You can configure your portal to allow actions like viewing, signing, uploading documents, paying invoices, and completing requests without requiring portal login

## Add portal access

You can enable client portal access and send invitations either in bulk or individually.

### In bulk for all contacts

To add Client portal access in bulk from **Clients > Accounts**:

1. Filter your account list by clicking **Filter**, select **Last login**, then choose **Offline** to see clients without portal access
2. Choose the clients, click **More actions**, then select **Edit login, notify, email sync**
3. Select **Enable for all contacts** from the drop-down next to **Login** to enable portal access
4. Select the **Send invitations** checkbox
5. Add an optional message for the invitation email
6. Click **Save** to invite clients in bulk

### Individually for selected contacts

To enable portal access for specific contacts:

1. Navigate to the **Info** tab of the client profile
2. In the **Contacts** section, check the **Login** box next to each contact needing portal access
3. Type a personalized message in the pop-up field, then click **Send**

**Note:** If one contact is linked to multiple accounts, grant portal access separately for each account.

## Send invitations to users pending activation

A user is pending activation if:

- The **Login** box is checked but notification hasn't been sent
- Notification was sent but the user hasn't logged in yet

To resend invitations, click the three-dots icon next to the user's email in the **Info** tab, then click **Send invitation**.

Alternatively, go to **Clients > Accounts** and select the **Pending activation** tab. Here you can:

- View email addresses, account names, invitation sent dates, and who sent the invite
- Select users and click **Resend to select** to resend invitations
- Click **Cancel invites to selected** to revoke pending invitations

## How do I know when new clients have registered on TaxDome?

Once a client activates their account, all assigned team members receive a notification in **Inbox+** and an email. Click the **Go to** link to review the new client profile. New registrations also appear on the Activity feed page.

## Do my clients have portal access?

Check client activation status in the **ID** column of your **Clients > Accounts** list:

- **Green:** Client account is **Activated**—at least one user can log in
- **Yellow:** Client account is **Pending activation**—invitation sent but client hasn't activated yet
- **Gray:** Client account is **Not activated**—invitation not sent and no active users

To check a specific contact's access in their profile **Info** tab:

- The client lacks access if the **Login** box is unchecked
- The client lacks access if the **Login** box is checked but pending activation
