# Send one-time invoices

## Overview

Create and send one-time invoices to charge clients for services. Invoices can be sent to individual clients, multiple clients at once, or generated through proposals, time entries, or pipeline automation.

**Note:** Only firm owners or team members assigned to an account with "Manage invoices" access rights can create and send invoices.

## Send a one-time invoice to one client

1. Create an invoice using one of these methods:
   - Click **New** and select **Invoice**
   - Open a client account, go to the **Invoices** tab, and click **New invoice** (the account auto-fills)
2. Enter the invoice amount or select a template
3. Complete the invoice details (see Invoice field reference for all options)
4. Click **Create** to send the invoice

Clients receive email and mobile app notifications upon receipt.

## Send one-time invoices to multiple clients

1. Go to **Account > Clients**, select checkboxes next to desired clients, then click **Create Invoice**
2. Enter the invoice amount or select a template
3. Complete the invoice details
4. Click **Create** to send to all selected clients

**Note:** You can send invoices to active or offline account statuses.

## Alternative ways to send invoices

- Create templates and use automation to send invoices
- Send invoices alongside proposals
- Generate one-time invoices from proposal services
- Create invoices from time entries

## Preview an invoice

Use the **Preview** option (top right) to see the exact PDF clients will receive. Click **Create & close** from preview mode to confirm. Access **Invoice settings** from preview to customize appearance and white-label invoices.

## Set up invoice reminders

Enable automatic follow-up emails for unpaid invoices:

1. Toggle **Reminders** on when sending the invoice
2. Set **Days until next reminder** (default: 3 days)
3. Set **Number of reminders** (default: 1)

Update these settings later by editing the unpaid invoice. Manually resend invoices anytime using the three-dot menu and selecting **Send invoice by email**.

## Client view

Clients can access invoices through:

- Client portal or mobile app notifications
- Email notification with attached PDF invoice

Invoice status automatically changes from "Unpaid" to "Paid" when payment is received. View invoices from the client perspective using read-only account access.
