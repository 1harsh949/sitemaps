# What you can do in TaxDome

## Get oriented

Before exploring features, become acquainted with how TaxDome organizes itself and its terminology.

- **Ways to access TaxDome**: Choose your preferred method—web portal, desktop app, or mobile app—based on the task at hand.
- **Find your way around TaxDome**: Learn the main navigation elements used across all features.
- **TaxDome dictionary**: Reference definitions for terminology including pipelines, jobs, tasks, accounts, contacts, organizers, and related concepts.

## Try before going live

Explore TaxDome safely using test accounts before working with real client information.

- **Test client accounts**: Build one or two test accounts to preview the portal exactly as clients will experience it.

For team members, consult "Get started as a team member in TaxDome" for step-by-step daily workflows. Firm owners should review "Set up your TaxDome account as a firm owner" for configuration guidance.

## Organize your client base

Client structure affects findability, communication, and bulk operations later. Primary organizational tools include:

- **Contacts and accounts**: Clients exist as accounts containing one or more contacts.
- **Tags**: Categorize clients, accounts, jobs, and documents for filtering, automation, and group actions.
- **Custom fields**: Store firm-specific data not fitting standard fields—referral sources, fiduciary contacts, and tracked metrics.
- **Custom folder templates**: Preset folder structures automatically applied to new client accounts, ensuring consistent document organization.

## Run your work with pipelines

Pipelines transform recurring services into trackable, automatable workflows with substantial efficiency gains over time.

- **Pipelines explained**: Understand pipeline structure and their application to your work.
- **Pipelines, stages, jobs, tasks**: Learn hierarchical relationships—pipelines contain jobs, jobs advance through stages, tasks reside within jobs.
- **Automations**: Configure automated actions triggered as jobs progress through stages—sending invoices, delivering intake forms, requesting signatures, and messaging clients.

> **Tip**: Firm owners and administrators typically configure pipelines; team members primarily work within them.

## Bill and get paid

Manage the complete billing cycle—from engagement proposals through payment collection—integrated directly into the platform.

- **TaxDome Payments**: Built-in payment processing accepting card and ACH transactions, with adjustable fee structures and pay-on-behalf capabilities.
- **Invoicing overview**: Create single and recurring invoices, restrict document access to unpaid invoices, and automate billing through pipelines.
- **Proposals and engagement letters**: Send packaged proposals for client acceptance and signature with optional deposit collection.

## Collect, sign, and deliver documents

Handle all document activities in a unified space—gathering client information, preparing returns, obtaining signatures, and delivering completed work.

- **How docs work**: Document and folder structure, permission controls, and file movement between firm and client.
- **Tax-prep workflow**: Integrated capability connecting intake, preparation, signing, and delivery stages.
- **E-signatures**: Request and collect signatures on returns, engagement letters, and supporting documents.
- **Organizers**: Structured intake forms for client completion—tax questionnaires and multi-question information requests.

## Engage your clients

Client portal experience influences upload willingness, signature compliance, and payment promptness.

- **Customize your client portal**: Apply branding, control visible features, and refine messaging.
- **Introduce TaxDome to your clients**: Use provided templates and guidance to explain the portal and encourage adoption.

## Keep learning

Structured learning resources support every experience level beyond the Help Center.

- **Implementation support**: Hands-on assistance from TaxDome specialists for advanced setup needs.
- **TaxDome YouTube channel**: On-demand playlists featuring feature demonstrations, walkthroughs, and use-case videos.
- **TaxDome Community**: Connect with other firms, receive product notifications, and exchange workflows.
