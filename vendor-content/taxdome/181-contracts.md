# Send proposals

Send [proposals and engagement letters](https://help.taxdome.com/article/1229-proposals-overview) to clients. You can send them manually to one client, in bulk to multiple clients, or automatically via pipeline automations.

## Ways to send proposals

**Note:** Proposals can be sent by the firm owner, admins, and team members with the **Manage proposals** access right.

To send a proposal, you'll need a [proposal template](https://help.taxdome.com/article/117-custom-contract-templates). There are different ways to send proposals:

- Send to one client
- Send to multiple clients at once
- Send automatically via pipeline automations

## Send a proposal to one client

To send a proposal to a client:

1. Create a proposal using either of the ways:
   - Click **New** at the top left and select **Proposal**, then select the client account.
   - Open the client account, switch to the **Proposals & ELs** tab and click **New proposal & EL**. The account is pre-filled automatically.

2. Enter a name for the proposal, then click the **Template** drop-down to select a template. You can add [shortcodes](https://help.taxdome.com/article/594-shortcodes-basic-applying-shortcodes) to the name to individualize it.

3. Edit any template step if needed, add more pages, or turn them off. See [Proposal template field reference](https://help.taxdome.com/article/proposal-template-field-reference) for details on each option.

4. Optionally, to make the proposal visible to all team members working on a pipeline, click **Add job** at the bottom of the **General** step to link the proposal to a job. Linking to a job is only available when sending to one client.

5. Click **Send**.

**Note:** You can't send proposals to contacts linked to an account who don't have portal access (their **Login** needs to be turned on).

All users linked to the account with the **Signatory** checkbox enabled receive a notification by email and a push notification on the mobile app. Depending on the proposal settings, the proposal is considered signed when either at least one or all signatories have signed it.

A signature field is automatically added to the proposal. Once signed, the date is automatically added.

## Send proposals to multiple clients

To send the same proposal to multiple clients:

1. Go to **Clients > Accounts** from the left menu bar.

2. Select the checkboxes next to the target client accounts.

3. Click **More actions** in the menu bar, then select **Send proposal**.

All signatories on each selected account receive a notification by email and a push notification on the mobile app.

## Save proposal as draft

If you need more time to finalize a proposal before sending it to a client, save it as a draft. This way, you can prepare the proposal without triggering any client notifications and return to it later to continue editing. To do this, click **Save draft** instead of **Send**.

Drafts are saved to **Billing > Proposals & ELs** and to the **Proposals & ELs** tab of the client's account with the status **Draft**.

**Tip:** To find all draft proposals quickly, use filters: set **Status = Draft**.

### Send proposal from draft

To send the proposal from the draft:

1. Click the draft name.

2. Make any changes.

3. Click **Send**.

All signatories on the account receive a notification by email and a push notification on the mobile app.

## Set up proposal reminders

[Reminders](https://help.taxdome.com/article/119-automatic-reminders) are automatic follow-up emails that nudge clients who haven't signed a proposal yet.

To turn them on, toggle on **Reminders** when creating or sending the proposal, then set:

- **Days until next reminder** — how often the reminder repeats (default: 3)
- **Number of reminders** — how many reminders to send in total (default: 1)

You can update these settings later by [editing](https://help.taxdome.com/article/495-contracts-how-to-view-edit-print-delete#2) the pending proposal.

To resend a reminder manually at any time, click the three dots to the right of the proposal in the list and select **Resend**.

## Client view

Each user with the **Signatory** checkbox enabled receives a notification about a new proposal. Clients can see and open proposals in different ways:

- **Client portal or mobile app** — under the **Waiting for action** section or on the **Proposals & ELs** page.
- **Email notification** — via the link in the email.

Clients then interact with the proposal (for example, they can authorize a secure payment).

When the client submits the proposal, its status changes from **Pending** to **Partially signed** (if multiple signers are required) or **Signed**, and you receive a notification.

To see the sent proposal from the client's perspective, [access the read-only view](https://help.taxdome.com/article/221-access-the-read-only-view-of-client-account).
