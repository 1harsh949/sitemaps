# Workflow explained

## Workflow at a glance

TaxDome Workflow improves operational efficiency by establishing repeatable processes and automating routine tasks along with client communications. The system relies on three core components—pipelines, jobs, and tasks—working together to streamline your business operations.

## Pipelines

Pipelines represent established work procedures consisting of sequential steps or stages. They help you track progress on similar assignments across different clients while moving work toward completion smoothly.

Operating like a kanban board, pipelines function through:

1. **Stages** — steps in a work procedure that jobs advance through upon completion
2. **Jobs** — individual client projects (such as a tax return) that progress through the pipeline
3. **Automations** — mechanisms triggering independent actions like task creation or client communications

## Stages & automations

Pipelines consist of stages representing the different steps required for job completion. For example, a tax return pipeline might include stages like "Review Docs," "Prepare Engagement Letter," "Prepare Return," and "Review."

Each stage can include automations that simplify routine work. When a job enters a new stage, automatic actions trigger—such as creating tasks for team members or sending communications to clients.

## Jobs

Jobs represent individual services clients pay for, flowing through pipelines designed for specific service types (for example, a 1040 tax return). Each job can be compared to a single trip through a pipeline for a given client.

Key job characteristics include:

- Jobs advance through pipeline stages manually or automatically.
- Custom statuses can indicate blockers without moving the job to a different stage.
- Jobs may contain linked elements like organizers, tasks, messages, and invoices.
- Jobs can move between pipelines as circumstances change.

## Tasks

Tasks are to-do items performed by you and your team members. You can create multiple tasks within a job and assign them to different team members. For instance, within a single tax preparation job, you might create separate tasks for preparing different tax forms, enabling simultaneous completion by various staff members.
