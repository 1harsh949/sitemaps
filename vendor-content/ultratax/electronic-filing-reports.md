# Electronic filing reports

Occasionally, federal and state taxing authorities may experience electronic filing processing issues such as delayed acknowledgements or down-time for processing center hardware maintenance. We report any known processing delay issues and provide e-file tips via the **Alerts** and **Electronic filing and other product news** links in the Notices section of the UltraTax CS homepage.

Also in the Notices section, the **Electronic filing transmission status - Live!** link shows electronic files pending transmission, transmitted to agency, or accepted/rejected by agency but awaiting retrieval of acknowledgements.

Per-return pricing and electronic filing fee reports are available online at [cs.thomsonreuters.com/myaccount/prpelf](http://www.cs.thomsonreuters.com/myaccount/prpelf) (after signing in to your Web Account).
