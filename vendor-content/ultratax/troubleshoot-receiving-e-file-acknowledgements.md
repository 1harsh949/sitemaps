# Troubleshoot receiving e-file acknowledgments

You should receive federal acknowledgments from the IRS within 24 to 48 hours from transmitting the return via CS Connect. Extra processing time may occur around deadlines. UltraTax CS automatically re-sends e-file acknowledgments when there has been no activity for over 24 hours. If the client status in the Electronic Filing Status screen shows transmission to CS or the taxing agency after 48 hours, follow these steps.

## IRS acknowledgments

1. **Retrieve acknowledgements via CS Connect**
   - Select the CS Connect button in the UltraTax CS toolbar
   - In the **Option** tab, select the **Electronic filing: Retrieve acknowledgements** switch and set it to **Yes**
   - Select the **Connect** button

2. **Verify that the client's TIN or client ID has not changed** since the return was transmitted
   - If it has changed, revert to the original information and retrieve acknowledgments again via CS Connect

3. **Contact the IRS at 866-255-0654 or the state tax agency**
   - State taxing agency contact information is available in the [State Electronic Filing Guide](https://cs.thomsonreuters.com/support/reference/elf/)
   - Note that taxing agencies may experience delays issuing acknowledgments, and batched processing can cause varying acknowledgment speeds

4. **Chat with Triva** (Thomson Reuters Intelligent Virtual Assistant) to verify status. Provide:
   - Your name
   - Your firm ID
   - Your email address
   - UltraTax CS version year
   - Client IDs
   - Whether checking federal, state, or both acknowledgments

5. **If using a third-party application vendor** to transmit electronic returns, contact your vendor directly

## BSA e-filing system acknowledgments

For FinCEN Form 114 (FBAR) returns, the BSA E-Filing System processes files and transmits acknowledgments to Thomson Reuters within 1 business day. Acknowledgments are then available for download via CS Connect.
